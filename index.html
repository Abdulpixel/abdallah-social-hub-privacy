Privacy Policy for Abdallah Social Hub

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Abdallah Social Hub is a social media management platform designed to help users connect supported social media accounts, create content, publish posts, schedule content, and review available analytics.

Information We Collect

When you connect a supported social media platform, Abdallah Social Hub may receive account information made available through that platform's official APIs, such as account identifiers, profile information, managed pages or organizations, published content information, and analytics data.

Authentication and Access Tokens

Authentication tokens received from connected platforms are used only to provide requested platform functionality. Tokens are not intentionally exposed publicly or included in public URLs.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide features such as account connection, publishing, scheduling, account management, and analytics for supported social media platforms.

Third-Party Platforms

Abdallah Social Hub may integrate with third-party platforms such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and other supported services. Use of those platforms is also subject to their own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

Data Sharing

Abdallah Social Hub does not sell personal information. Data is only shared with third-party platforms when necessary to perform user-requested actions through their official APIs.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and platform data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as necessary to provide connected platform functionality or as required for legitimate technical and operational purposes.

User Control

Users may disconnect supported social media integrations from Abdallah Social Hub. Users may also manage or revoke permissions through the relevant third-party platform where available.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Abdallah Social Hub develops or adds new integrations and features. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Abdallah Social Hub, please contact the application administrator through the official communication channel associated with the service.
